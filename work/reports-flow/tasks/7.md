## Required Skills

Before executing the task, load:
- `/skill:code-writing` - [skills/code-writing/SKILL.md](~/.claude/skills/code-writing/SKILL.md)

## Description

Add recipient planning for captains, trackers, admin, and Sitnikov using team/tracker scope rules. This isolates role-safe routing before any messages or documents are sent.

## What to do

- Add recipient planning for team summaries, team PDFs, full summary, and group comparison.
- Enforce captain, tracker, admin, and Alexander Sitnikov scopes from the approved user-spec.
- Keep group comparison admin/Sitnikov-only.
- Represent missing chat ids as delivery-plan problems without sending messages.
- Do not send Telegram messages/documents in this task.
